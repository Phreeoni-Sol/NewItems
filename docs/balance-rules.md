# Balance Rules

## JP mastery targets

- Tier I: 2,800–3,500 JP
- Tier II: 4,000–5,000 JP
- Tier III: 5,500–6,500 JP
- Tier IV: 8,000–10,500 JP

## CT

Custom CT manipulation is capped at +40 / -40 CT per unit per round.

## Hard control

After suffering Stop, Exile, Stun or an equivalent hard control, a unit receives temporary
Control Resistance.

## Boss handling

Bosses should:
- receive shorter debuff duration;
- resist diseases;
- resist CT loss;
- be immune to Exile;
- resist execute effects;
- accept at most 2 Blood Marks.

## Resource-specific restrictions

### Hémomancien
- max 3 Blood Marks per target
- life steal target: <= 30% max HP restored per action

### Sanguelame
- self-damage cannot generate Fury

### Artificier
- max 4 active devices
- chain explosion damage should decay

### Conducteur d’Orage
- each chain bounce loses ~20% power

### Spirite
- max 5 souls consumed by a single ability

### Arbitre
- one Law active per Arbitre

### Weather
- one major weather state active at a time

### Parangon
- inherited skill power:
  - Tier I/II ~80%
  - Tier III ~75%
  - Tier IV ~70%
